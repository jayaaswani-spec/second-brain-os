description: "A relentless interview to sharpen a plan or design that also records the project's terms and decisions as they land: CONTEXT.md and ADRs in an app repo, glossary.md and decisions/ in a knowledge project. Use for \"grill me on this feature\" in an app repo, or when a spec will follow and the project's vocabulary matters."
---

Run a `/grilling` session, using the `/domain-modeling` skill. Recording terms and decisions as they land is part of the interview, not the "acting on it" that grilling holds back until we agree; offer a decision record as soon as the decision lands. In an app repo, `CONTEXT.md` and `docs/adr/` live at the repo root, whatever project owns it.

Asking for this skill, or asking to keep track of the terms, is the permission to write `glossary.md`, `decisions/`, `CONTEXT.md` and `docs/adr/` during the interview, and nothing else in the project. If you got here from a plain "grill me" with no ask to record anything, ask once before the first write.
